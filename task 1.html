Explore Our Impact

Project Overview & Objectives

Core Objectives

Project GreenReach aims to mitigate urban heat islands and improve local biodiversity by planting native trees and establishing community-managed gardens.

  • Environmental Clean-up: Revitalize 3 neglected urban lots.
  • Green Canopy: Plant over 150 native trees and shrubs.
  • Education: Host 4 community workshops on sustainable urban agriculture.

Beneficiaries & Outcomes

The primary beneficiaries are the 1,200 residents of the Oakwood District, who previously lacked immediate access to safe, clean public green spaces.

Implementation Stages & Timeline

Stage 1

Site Assessment & Permitting

Weeks 1 - 2

Soil testing, securing municipal permits, and conducting neighborhood survey assessments to gauge community needs.

Stage 2

Clearance & Infrastructure

Weeks 3 - 4

Debris removal, installing eco-friendly irrigation systems, and laying down organic topsoil paths.

Stage 3

Planting & Community Build

Weeks 5 - 6

Volunteers planted native tree varieties, erected raised garden beds, and installed community benches.
