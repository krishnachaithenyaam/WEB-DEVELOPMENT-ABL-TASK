Healthy Eating Tips

Small changes can make a big difference.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drink water regularly throughout the day. Your hydration needs depend on activity and climate.

🥦 Eat More Vegetables

Include a variety of colorful vegetables and fruits to get essential vitamins and minerals.

🍚 Choose Balanced Meals

Combine whole grains, protein, vegetables, and healthy fats for a balanced diet.

🍬 Limit Added Sugar

Reduce sugary drinks and sweets. Choose whole fruits for natural sweetness.

⏰ Eat Regularly

Plan regular meals and snacks according to your hunger, routine, and nutritional needs.

🥜 Choose Healthy Snacks

Try fruits, nuts, yogurt, or roasted chickpeas instead of highly processed snacks.
